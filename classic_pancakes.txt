Title: Classic Pancakes

Serving: 1 person

Ingredients:
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup milk
- 1 large egg
- 2 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions:
1. In a bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
2. In another bowl, mix milk, egg, and melted butter.
3. Combine wet and dry ingredients until just mixed (do not overmix).
4. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and grease lightly.
5. Pour 1/4 cup batter for each pancake and cook until bubbles form.
6. Flip and cook the other side until golden brown.
7. Serve warm with syrup or toppings of choice.
