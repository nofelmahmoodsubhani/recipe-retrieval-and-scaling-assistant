Title: Chicken Curry

Serving: 1 person

Ingredients:
- 500g chicken (cut into pieces)
- 2 onions, chopped
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon ginger paste
- 2 tablespoons curry powder
- 1 cup coconut milk
- 2 tablespoons oil
- Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:
1. Heat oil in a pan and sauté onions until golden.
2. Add garlic and ginger paste; cook for 1 minute.
3. Add chicken and cook until lightly browned.
4. Add curry powder and stir for 1-2 minutes.
5. Add tomatoes and cook until soft.
6. Pour in coconut milk and simmer for 20 minutes until chicken is cooked.
7. Season with salt and pepper; serve with rice.
