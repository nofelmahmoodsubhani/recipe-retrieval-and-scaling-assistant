Title: Spaghetti Carbonara

Serving: 1 person

Ingredients:
- 200g spaghetti
- 100g pancetta
- 2 large eggs
- 50g grated Parmesan cheese
- Salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions:
1. Cook spaghetti in salted water until al dente.
2. In a separate pan, fry pancetta until crispy.
3. Beat eggs in a bowl and mix with Parmesan cheese.
4. Drain pasta and immediately toss with pancetta.
5. Remove from heat, add egg-cheese mixture, and stir quickly to create a creamy sauce.
6. Season with salt and pepper before serving.
